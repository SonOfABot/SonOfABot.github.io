# Pwn.college Challenges

Here you'll find my solutions and write-ups for [pwn.college](https://pwn.college) practice challenges. Unlike traditional CTFs, pwn.college is built around dojos, structured skill trees covering binary exploitation, reverse engineering, and increasingly, adversarial machine learning and model evasion.

Each write-up here covers not just the solve, but the reasoning behind it, the algorithms involved, and where relevant, a comparison of alternative approaches.
